The Nandy

The Nandy (rhymes with “candy”) is named after me (my last name is Nandor). It is given out to people who tell lies through the media to manipulate the public.

The Verfy

The Verfy (rhymes with “murphy”) is named after the book’s title — Don’t Trust: Verify — but some social media platforms disallow the word “Verify” to be used in account names, so it was shortened to “DontTrustVerfy.” It is given out to people who expose liars who are eligible for The Nandy.
